# Personal CFO Terms of Use

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Personal CFO is a private, self-hosted application intended solely for the operator's personal, non-commercial use with accounts owned or controlled by that operator.

## Permitted use

The application may retrieve read-only account information after the operator grants consent through the relevant bank. It may store that information and produce deterministic personal-finance calculations and reconciliation results. It does not initiate payments, execute trades, or provide access to another person's accounts.

## Financial information

Personal CFO is an informational record-keeping tool, not financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Calculations may be incomplete or unavailable when provider data is missing, stale, or unreconciled. The operator remains responsible for decisions made using the application.

## Third-party services

Bank connectivity depends on Enable Banking and the selected bank. Their availability, consent requirements, response content, and terms are outside Personal CFO's control. Access may stop when consent expires, is revoked, or a provider service is unavailable.

## Security responsibilities

The operator is responsible for protecting the host, local login, private keys, backups, and connected-bank credentials. Bank authentication must be completed only through the bank-controlled authorization flow; Personal CFO does not collect the operator's bank password or Smart-ID credentials.

## Availability and changes

The application is provided for the operator's own use without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or error-free provider data. These terms and the application's behavior may be updated as the private deployment evolves. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

## Ending access

The operator may stop using Personal CFO, disconnect the bank integration, and revoke consent through the bank or Enable Banking at any time. Retained local data remains subject to the Personal CFO Privacy Notice and the application's supported deletion controls.
