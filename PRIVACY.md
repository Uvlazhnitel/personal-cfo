# Personal CFO Privacy Notice

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Personal CFO is a private, self-hosted, single-user application for viewing and analysing the operator's own financial information. It is not offered to the public and is not used for commercial account aggregation.

## Information processed

Personal CFO may process the operator's bank account identifiers, balances, transaction records, consent and connection status, investment information, financial settings, and calculated financial metrics. Local authentication and security metadata are also processed to protect access to the application.

## Purpose and access

The information is used only to provide the operator with personal financial records, reconciliation, and deterministic financial calculations. Personal CFO does not sell financial information, use it for advertising, or provide it to other users.

Enable Banking and the selected bank process information as necessary to establish consent and provide read-only account information under their respective terms and privacy notices.

## Storage and retention

Data is stored in the operator-controlled Personal CFO deployment. Provider credentials and private keys are kept outside the application database. Sensitive provider sessions and raw receipts are encrypted at rest. Encrypted raw provider receipts are scheduled to expire after 30 days; normalized financial records and audit information may be retained until the operator disconnects or deletes the relevant data in accordance with the application's supported deletion controls.

## Security and operator controls

Access is restricted by local authentication. The operator may revoke bank consent through the bank or Enable Banking and may disconnect the integration in Personal CFO. Disconnecting stops further access but does not silently alter financial facts already retained for audit and reconciliation.

## Contact

Privacy questions should be sent to the data-protection email registered for the Personal CFO application in the Enable Banking Control Panel.
